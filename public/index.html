Genomics & Gene Expression

Genome browsers, sequences & expression data

Genomics

ANISEED

The community's flagship model-organism database: genomes, gene models, in situ expression, cis-regulatory data, functional perturbations, a genome browser and single-cell RNA-seq across many ascidian species.

aniseed.fr
Genomics

GHOST

Kyoto University's integrated Ciona intestinalis / robusta resource: genomic and cDNA/EST sequences, the KY21 gene models on the HT assembly, expression images, BLAST and a genome browser.

ghost.zool.kyoto-u.ac.jp/download_ht
Marine models

MARIMBA

Marine Invertebrate Models Database (Villefranche-sur-Mer): genome browser, BLAST, gene/transcript/protein search, spatio-temporal expression, developmental-stage and anatomy ontologies, and molecular tools (morpholinos, qPCR, CRISPR). Its tunicate model is the colonial ascidian Botryllus schlosseri — allorecognition, whole-body regeneration and stem-cell biology.

marimba.obs-vlfr.fr
BLAST server

Octopus Database

Genome database and bioinformatics server of the LBDV (Villefranche-sur-Mer): BLAST databases, JBrowse genome browser and sequence/gene-prediction tools for marine invertebrates — ascidians, thaliaceans, amphioxus, cnidarians, echinoderms and molluscs — including the public Botryllus schlosseri BLAST and its 2025 genome annotation download. Some databases are access-restricted.

octopus.obs-vlfr.fr
Comparative

TUNOME

A comparative tunicate genome database (Keio) with functional annotation of gene models and ortholog/transcriptomic analyses across 35+ tunicate species. New (2025).

ciona.bpni.bio.keio.ac.jp/Tunome
Morphology & Anatomy

Anatomical atlases, ontologies & imaging

3D Morphology

MorphoNet

Interactive web-based 3D morphodynamic browser for visualizing and sharing multi-scale morphological datasets, onto which gene expression or quantitative data can be projected — widely used for ascidian embryo atlases.

morphonet.org
Ontology

TunicAnatO

Tunicate anatomical & developmental ontology for Ciona: 37 developmental stages and ~200 anatomical entities from cell lineage to organ level, with time-lapse stereomicroscopy.

chordate.bpni.bio.keio.ac.jp/tunicanato
Comparative 3D

RAMNe

Comparative 3D digital-image resource of ascidian embryos (Ciona robusta, Ascidiella aspersa, Styela clava) with interactive Z-section and rotation viewers for cross-species comparison.

chordate.bpni.bio.keio.ac.jp/RAMNe
