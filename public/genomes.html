Publicly available tunicate genome assemblies, grouped by phylogeny and family. Click any species to expand its assemblies and jump straight to NCBI.

— assemblies — species 3 classes — chromosome-level — not public yet

Pick a class, then click any species (▸) to see its assemblies. Gene models are tied to a specific assembly: the expanded Gene model column shows where annotations exist — NCBI (RefSeq), TUNOME / GHOST, and/or ANISEED — and the Source column links to the assembly on NCBI, or ANISEED / GHOST / other repositories where there is no NCBI accession. Search filters within the selected class.

The Level column is the assembly level reported by NCBI Datasets (Chromosome Scaffold Contig); for the few assemblies with no NCBI accession it is taken from the source publication. NCBI calls an assembly Chromosome as soon as any sequence is placed on a chromosome, so the percentage on the chip says how much of the assembly actually sits on chromosomes — Chromosome 68% (Ciona KH, 1,257 unplaced scaffolds) is a very different genome from Chromosome 99%. A species carries the chromosome-level badge when at least one of its assemblies is chromosome-level — tick Chromosome-level only to list just those species.

Species in red have no public genome — either sequencing in progress (no assembly yet) or assembled (assembled in a lab, not released). Red does not mean off-limits — always expand and read the entry before assuming a genome is out of reach. Some owners have told us they are happy to share on request, and the entry says so; all of them give a contact, and links to the paper or the raw reads where those are public. Expand for what is known: estimated genome size, sampling site, assembly state, publication and contact. Where a species already has a public assembly and an unreleased one, the unreleased dataset appears as a red row inside that species' expansion — it is never counted in the assembly totals above. ℹ️Genomes without an NCBI assembly

Some genomes have no GCA accession but do have gene models. They are still listed above — their Source column links to ANISEED, GHOST or another repository instead of NCBI. These include Molgula occidentalis / M. oculata / M. occulta (tail-loss evo-devo models), Botrylloides leachii (regeneration) and Corella inflata. The Ciona robusta / Type-A GHOST HT assembly does have an accession (GCA_009617815.2, listed under C. intestinalis), but its KY21 gene models are only at GHOST.

Not yet sequenced at all: any Pyrosoma (pyrosomes) or Doliolum (doliolids) — no public genome exists.
