ℹ️Genomes without an NCBI assembly

Some genomes have no GCA accession but do have gene models. They are still listed above — their Source column links to ANISEED, GHOST or another repository instead of NCBI. These include Molgula occidentalis / M. oculata / M. occulta (tail-loss evo-devo models), Botrylloides leachii (regeneration), Corella inflata, and the Ciona robusta / Type-A GHOST HT gene-model reference (listed under C. intestinalis).

Not yet sequenced at all: Salpa fusiformis, and any Pyrosoma (pyrosomes) or Doliolum (doliolids) — no public genome exists.
